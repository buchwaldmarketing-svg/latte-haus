Our story

A coffee shop where everyone feels like family.

Latte Haus was born from a simple idea: Oak Cliff deserves a coffee shop where everyone feels like family. Founded by Emily, a passionate young entrepreneur, we opened our doors on W Kiest Blvd to bring handcrafted drinks, warm energy and a beautifully designed space to the neighborhood.

Whether you're grabbing your morning cold brew, settling in for a midday matcha, or catching up with friends over a seasonal special, this is your space. We're not just making coffee. We're building community, one cup at a time.
